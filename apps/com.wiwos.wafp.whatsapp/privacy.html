Privacy Notice: WIWOS AI Force Platform (WhatsApp)

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This notice explains how WIWOS TECHNOLOGIES SDN. BHD. (“WIWOS”, “we”), a company incorporated in Malaysia, handles your personal data when you chat with the WIWOS AI Force Platform (“WAFP”) on WhatsApp. WAFP is an AI assistant that helps staff describe how they do their work, so that WIWOS can design useful tools for the business that invited you (your “employer”).

In case of any difference between the English version and a translation, the English version prevails.

1. What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp mobile number and the name, role and language your employer registered for you.
  • The messages you send to WAFP: text, voice notes, photos and videos, and the replies WAFP sends you.
  • The workflow descriptions we build from your messages (steps, systems used, how often, ideas to improve).
  • A record of your consent (date, time and the version of the text you agreed to) and of any request to stop.
  • Technical records needed to run and secure the service (for example message times and error logs).

2. Why we use it

  • To talk with you on WhatsApp and understand how your work is done.
  • To prepare workflow drafts that WIWOS developers and your employer review.
  • To keep the service secure, to fix problems and to keep proof of your consent.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers that help us

To run WAFP we use service providers that process data on our instructions: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform, which carries your messages), Anthropic (an AI language model that writes WAFP’s replies), ElevenLabs (converts voice notes to text) and Amazon Web Services (hosting in Malaysia). Some of these providers may process data outside Malaysia. We send them only what is needed to answer you. Each provider also has its own privacy notice.

4. Who can see your information

The WIWOS team that builds and runs the solution for your employer, and the administrators of your employer who registered you. Information of one employer is kept separate from every other employer.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above (generally while your employer uses WAFP), and then delete or anonymise it. You can ask us to delete your data sooner at any time.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You may stop at any time by telling WAFP to stop in the chat, or by contacting us.
  • You may withdraw your consent, and ask to access, correct or delete your personal data.
  • If you stop, WAFP will not message you again, except to confirm that you stopped.

7. Security

We protect data with access controls, encrypted connections and encrypted storage of secrets, and we keep each employer’s data separate. No system is completely secure, and we will act on any incident as the law requires.

8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice and will publish the new version at this address with a new date.
